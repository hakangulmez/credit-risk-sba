# V1 issues — 8 October 2026

Static review of `v1-original` (`1a4e1ac`); no models rerun. Notebook references below use stable cell IDs.

- **Outcome selection and censoring.** `scripts/data_processing.py:48–49` drops missing status and labels every non-`P I F` status as default, without a charge-off horizon. The approval-date split reported as August 2006 does not equalize follow-up. The [original paper, §3.3](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/10691898.2018.1434342), confirms that the 1987–2014 extract includes only known outcomes: long-term surviving loans can be missing while early charge-offs enter. `Term` can therefore reflect outcome-selection effects. A 36-month age filter cannot restore omitted survivors; the exact administrative cutoff and complete cohort denominator remain unverified.
- **Feature timing.** Cell `a9c25142` uses `DisbursementGross`, which is unavailable at approval; its availability at first disbursement is also undocumented. The dictionary describes jobs created/retained without a measurement date. `Term` means loan months, but its original-versus-updated vintage is unspecified; `TermBucket` and `IsRealEstate` inherit this ambiguity. These concerns need evidence, not assumptions that every field leaks.
- **Validation leakage.** Cell `e04fc5fd` fits categorical encoders on train plus test, and trains baseline LightGBM on its own validation rows. Cell `b9ef55ee` uses test labels for final early stopping. Cell `eb2fe5a2` calibrates baseline LightGBM on previously fitted rows. ElasticNet's ordinary five-fold CV is not forward validation; its upstream scaler also sees all inner folds (`15930308`).
- **Loss claims.** Cell `ae3b4b8d` estimates LGD from all-period outcomes and calls realized accepted-loan default frequency × LGD × summed guarantee amount “expected loss.” `SBA_Appv` is a guarantee, not observed EAD. The report's equal-acceptance claim compares unequal approval rates.
- **Diagnostics.** PSI drops test observations outside training extrema (`625d03dd`); urban/rural differences do not establish protected-group fairness. Neither temporal patterns nor SHAP establish causal effects.
- **Reproducibility.** README retains another repository/zip name, contradictory Python requirements, a false fully-pinned claim, and inconsistent section labels. Requirements use ranges, without a lock; the fetcher lacks timeout/checksum checks. Preserve old artifacts as history, not performance targets.
