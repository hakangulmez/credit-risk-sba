## Field verification and timing

Present: approval date/FY, first-disbursement date, status, payoff date, charge-off date, gross charge-off balance, total approval, guaranteed approval, lender identifiers/names, borrower state/ZIP, project state/county, NAICS, loan term, application jobs, business age/type, processing method, initial rate, revolver and collateral indicators. `LocationID` is SBA's **lender ID**, not a loan ID; use immutable source-file/row coordinates for row lineage. `BankName` names the bank **currently assigned** to the loan, so lender fields are excluded from approval-time predictors.

`InitialInterestRate` is explicitly at approval. `JobsSupported` is created plus retained jobs reported by the lender on the loan application: expected, unaudited estimates, not realized employment. `TermInMonths` does not prove original rather than modified term. `CollateralInd` does not disclose collateral type or historical timing. Other origination descriptors are retrospective A* assumptions, fully listed in the protocol; no real-time vintage claim is warranted. Historical business-age/NAICS category changes require fixed mappings and missing/unseen categories, not post-test selection.

Absent: **DisbursementGross**, current drawn balance, recoveries, a stable unique loan ID, **UrbanRural**, and the extract's separate NoEmp/CreateJob/RetainedJob variables. FOIA does contain **PaidInFullDate**, so the old extract's absent-payoff rationale for rejecting survival methods no longer applies. Fixed-horizon prediction remains the approved main estimand. The requested urban/rural diagnostic is unsupported in the primary schema; a verified external crosswalk would be future work.

## EAD, LGD and guarantee allocation: approved definitions

**EAD = GrossApproval; full-disbursement proxy; CCF = 100% (conservative utilization assumption).** Put this exact qualification in every loss table and distinguish term loans from revolving lines (`RevolverStatus` is N/Y in all observed CSVs, despite the dictionary's 0/1 description). Unused revolving commitments commonly draw less than full approval, but no drawn-share distribution is observed in FOIA. Full utilization is not measured outstanding principal or a universal upper bound.

FOIA `GrossChargeOffAmount` includes **guaranteed plus nonguaranteed** charged-off balance. Estimate the training-default LGD proxy against **the same GrossApproval denominator**, with the protocol's fixed segment pooling. Ratios above one are observed; keep the gross charge-off/approval primary proxy uncapped, flag it, and show the predeclared cap-at-one sensitivity. It is not net-of-recovery economic LGD, and its snapshot amount may differ from balance at the initial event date.

Guarantee share is `SBAGuaranteedApproval / GrossApproval`. Multiply total model EL by this share and its complement for SBA/lender allocation; do not use guarantee dollars as total EAD or double-apply the share inside LGD. This is a pro-rata allocation assumption, not actual guarantee claims, recoveries or public fiscal cost. Invalid guarantee shares become missing predictors; affected loans remain in PD, with loss allocation reported for the valid-share subset.

- **FHFA:** [official datasets](https://www.fhfa.gov/data/hpi/datasets), **All-Transactions / States / Not Seasonally Adjusted**, not the purchase-only state summary. [Direct CSV](https://www.fhfa.gov/hpi/download/quarterly_datasets/hpi_at_state.csv): **187,977 bytes**, SHA-256 `b6bf3687c230c2ad008657fdb0c2b7489b5f99b6d1193f72c46c68d88df83d90`. [MACRO_SERIES.md](docs/MACRO_SERIES.md) records 51 verified direct-agency identifiers and the scope of metadata-only FRED verification; URL existence alone is not a check of numerical equality across distributors.

## Qualified extract fallback and EAD sensitivity

[Historical review](docs/DATA_SOURCE_CHECK.md) verifies Li, Mickel & Taylor (2018), DOI [10.1080/10691898.2018.1434342](https://doi.org/10.1080/10691898.2018.1434342), the article's CC BY 4.0 and LRZ hosting terms, but **not the mirror dataset's licence or precise cutoff**. No old CSV/full ZIP was fetched. FOIA remains primary; the absent disbursed amount is resolved by the user-authorized approval proxy, so it does not trigger fallback.

If a genuinely necessary FOIA field later proves unavailable, freeze a documented extract cutoff and verified original term before any fallback. Restrict to loans with `DisbursementDate + original Term ≤ cutoff` **and** a complete 36-month window, using one eligibility rule regardless of status. Later cohorts will then disproportionately contain shorter-term loans. Verify that every matured loan, including unresolved/problem loans, is represented; contractual maturity alone cannot remove selection if matured active loans remain omitted. Without licence/cutoff/original-term/coverage evidence, fallback is **unsupported**, not a primary-population replacement. Do not choose a cutoff or restrict terms after observing scores.

At G2, after source rights/cutoff verification, report `DisbursementGross / GrAppv` distributions on that qualified population by term/revolving, fixed approval-size bands and disbursement year. The protocol fixes the segment-median CCF scenario and explicit ratio handling. This is an **assumption-based EAD sensitivity only**, subject to selected-outcome sample and amount-vintage limitations; no record linkage or claim of FOIA drawn shares is made.
